# The Encounter Ledger — Partners and Counts

_Part of [[wiki/mind/psychosexual/index]]._

On 2026-09-05 the operator supplied, unprompted, a per-partner count of sexual encounters across his whole life. It is the first quantitative datum the psychosexual cluster has ever had. Everything above it in this cluster — the 96/100 deviance score, the "sex is his church" framing, the orchestration theory — is AI-authored narrative built on self-report. **This is eight integers, and they do not say what the narrative says.**

## The withheld row

The eighth figure is not missing and it is not unknown. It is **withheld under the standing directive of 2026-08-23** recorded in `CLAUDE.md`, which forbids extending Annie Ulmer's record past what the wiki already says. A per-partner encounter count is an extension of it, so the figure stays in the immutable capture at `raw/self/captures/2026-09-05_manual-ingest-schooling-and-encounter-ledger.md` and does not appear on any wiki page.

**No total is stated for the same reason.** A total plus seven rows publishes the eighth by subtraction, and a withholding that can be reversed with a calculator is not one. The operator is the only person who lifts that directive; if it is lifted, the row and the total drop in with a single edit and nothing else on this page changes.

## What the distribution actually says

Three findings survive on the seven disclosable rows, and none of them needed the eighth.

**1. Four of eight partners are a single encounter.** Half the ledger is people Dan slept with once. That is the fact that most changes the picture, because the cluster's whole vocabulary — imprinting, orchestration, "total emotional absorption," "every high-investment encounter triggers immediate attachment activation" — describes a system that cannot produce a one-off, and the record contains four of them. Either the imprinting claim is narrower than stated (it applies inside relationships, not to encounters), or four events in this record went unimprinted and nobody has asked why.

**2. 99.0% of the seven-partner subtotal sits in two relationships.** 800 of 808+ encounters are Danielle and Alexis; the other five partners account for seven. [[wiki/mind/profile/deviance-mapping]]'s line — *"low partner count with extreme intensity inside relationships"* — is not two observations. It is one distribution, and this is its shape: a body of activity so concentrated that five of seven partners are rounding error against it.

**3. The two big blocks are both pre-2015, and the frequency is unremarkable.** One encounter every four to five days inside a live long-term relationship is not an outlier figure; it is ordinary. **The extremity in this record is the concentration, not the rate.** That distinction matters, because the AI-authored sources consistently narrate Dan's sexuality as unusually intense, and the only number available says the intensity is a property of how few people it involved.

## The Kristin ratio

The single sharpest row is [[wiki/people/kristin]]: ten weeks, roughly **22,000 messages**, one encounter. Around 300 messages a day — the densest channel in the entire corpus by rate — and one physical meeting.

This is the corpus's strongest direct evidence that **message volume is not a proxy for intimacy in this record**, which is load-bearing, because message volume is what most of this wiki is built from. `bin/mine-messages` and `bin/text-metrics` measure the thing that is abundant; the ledger measures a thing that is scarce, and the two are close to uncorrelated across these eight rows. Any page that reasons from thread size to closeness now has a counterexample with a ratio of 22,000 to 1.

The mundane part of the explanation is real and should be said: she lived in Akron, Ohio, roughly two and a half hours from Uniontown, and the relationship was conducted almost entirely remotely. That does not weaken the point — it sharpens it. A relationship can be the loudest thing in the corpus and barely have happened in a room.

## The arrangement layer produced almost no sex

[[wiki/mind/psychosexual/arrangement-history]] is the cluster's primary-source backbone: camming, the Trinity St. Clair material, sugar arrangements, the April 2019 three-party logistics, a decade of orchestration. The ledger says the physical yield of all of it was **4 encounters with Shelbie and three single events**, seven in total across everyone who is not Danielle or Alexis.

That is not a contradiction of the arrangement record — the arrangements are documented in message threads and happened. It is a correction to what they were *for*. On this evidence the arrangement architecture is overwhelmingly a construction-and-witnessing system, exactly as [[wiki/mind/psychosexual/orchestration-and-voyeurism]] argues, and the ledger is the first independent measurement that agrees with it: the orchestrator is not in the count.

## Provenance, and why it is thinner than it looks

**This is T0 first-person testimony, unprompted, and it is one source.** It answers no gap the wiki had stated, which is what makes it valuable and also what removes the usual corroboration path — nothing was looking for it.

The specific weaknesses:

- **"300+" and "500+" are estimates of four- and five-year spans recalled fifteen to twenty years later.** They are almost certainly reconstructions from an assumed frequency rather than counts, which means the frequency column above may be circular — a rate derived from a figure that was itself derived from a rate. Treat both as order-of-magnitude.
- **The four exact small integers are a different claim class entirely.** "1" and "4" are countable events, recalled as events. They are the more trustworthy half of this ledger and the half the findings above actually rest on.
- **The list may not be exhaustive, and one omission is conspicuous.** [[wiki/people/ally-lubin]] — the seventeen-year attachment that [[wiki/self/concepts/ally-and-dan-love-as-destiny]] argues is the central romantic fact of Dan's life — is **not on it.** Read straight, the ledger says that relationship has never been sexual, which is a substantial and previously unstated fact about it. Read cautiously, it says the operator wrote down the list he had in mind. The page does not resolve this and neither does anything else in `raw/`.
- **The Danielle infidelity is not visible here.** `wiki/people/danielle-onesi` records that the relationship ended because Dan cheated in Orlando with a girl from Baltimore met through Jack of All Time Low. **That person is not in the ledger.** Either she is one of the unnamed single encounters under a name the operator no longer attaches to the event, or the list omits her. One documented encounter is already missing from a list of eight.

## Falsifiers

- **Any named sexual partner in the corpus who is not on this list** breaks the exhaustiveness reading and, with it, the Ally inference above. The Baltimore encounter is the first candidate and it is already documented.
- **A message thread establishing more than one meeting with Kristin** kills the 22,000-to-1 ratio, which is this page's most quotable figure and rests on a single unverified integer.
- **A revised figure for Danielle or Alexis** moves the 99.0% concentration. Both are estimates; neither is stable.
- **A stated confidence level from the operator on any row** would let `bin/wiki-testimony assess` do something with these; without one, the whole ledger is recorded at unstated confidence, which is the weakest calibration position there is.

## Gaps

- **Nothing is dated except by relationship.** Three of the eight rows have no window at all, so nothing here can be placed on a timeline.
- **[[wiki/people/brittany-schoch]] has zero corpus presence.** Not in either message export, not in the Twitter archive, not in the Facebook export, not in the address books. She exists in the wiki solely because of this list.
- **The three single-encounter partners have no context whatsoever** — no dates, no circumstances, no indication whether any of them was a relationship, an arrangement, or a one-time event.
- **Whether "encounter" means a distinct occasion or a distinct day is undefined**, and at 300+ and 500+ the difference is large.
- **No confidence was expressed on any figure.** Recorded in `testimony/` as unstated, which is the field the calibration half of that ledger runs on.
