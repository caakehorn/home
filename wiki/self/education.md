# Education — The Whole Schooling Sequence

_Part of [[wiki/self/index]]._

Dan was put into school a year early and left it a year early too, and until 2026-09-05 the wiki recorded neither fact. What it had was a credential — *A.S. Recording Arts, Full Sail, top 5%* — attached to a person whose previous seventeen years of schooling appeared nowhere. This page is that front half.

**The sequence, in full:**

| Grades | Institution | School years (derived) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| K, 1 | St. John's Byzantine | 1993–95 |
| 2–6 | Hatfield Elementary | 1995–2000 |
| 7–8 | Laurel Highlands Middle School | 2000–02 |
| 9–11 | Laurel Highlands High School | 2002–05 |
| 12 (started, not finished) | Laurel Highlands School District | 2005–06, exited ~February 2006 |
| 12 (completed) | Keystone Online Charter School | 2006 |
| 4 semesters | Penn State Fayette | undated — see Gaps |
| A.S. Recording Arts | Full Sail University | Sep 2008 – Mar 2010 |

The school-year column is arithmetic, not testimony. The operator's account gives the institutions and the grades attached to each; the years follow from a November 1, 1988 birth date and the single stated fact that he started a year early. Everything in that column is therefore checkable and worth checking — see Gaps.

## The year early is the load-bearing fact

An ordinary Pennsylvania kindergarten cohort for a child born November 1, 1988 starts in September 1994, at five years and ten months. Dan started in September 1993, at four years and ten months. That single decision, made by his parents before he could weigh in on it, set the offset that runs through every other row of the table: **he was the youngest person in every classroom he sat in for thirteen years.**

The wiki already knew the consequence without knowing the cause. [[wiki/timeline/events/bald-eagle-cummings]] builds its entire reading of the seventh-grade humiliation on Dan being *"always a full year or more younger than his classmates"* compounded with being *"genetically a late bloomer"* and *"fully and completely yet to start the process of puberty"* — a twelve-year-old placed in a sexual situation he could not physically participate in, in a cohort of thirteen- and fourteen-year-olds. That page states the offset as a given. This one supplies the mechanism: two years at a small Byzantine Catholic parish school, entered ahead of schedule, and never corrected afterwards.

**It also fixes that event's date, which the page listed as an open gap.** Seventh grade for this sequence is Laurel Highlands Middle School in the 2000–01 school year, and the page's own guess — *"puts it around the 2000–01 school year"* — was right. The winter day inside it is still unfixed and probably always will be.

## February 2006: he left the year he was going to graduate

The dropout is the sharpest thing on this page, and its shape is not the ordinary one.

He started twelfth grade at Laurel Highlands in September 2005 and left around **Presidents' Day 2006** — the third Monday in February, which in 2006 fell on **February 20**. He was seventeen years and three months old. He then completed the credential through **Keystone Online Charter School**, a Pennsylvania cyber charter, the same year.

Three things about that are worth holding separately:

1. **He did not fail out; he left mid-senior-year and finished anyway.** The diploma exists. This is not a GED, not an abandonment, and not a story about being unable to do the work — it is a story about being unable to be in the building. Whatever the wiki eventually concludes about the dropout, it has to account for a person who walked out in February and still submitted the coursework.
2. **The timing sits directly downstream of the November 2005 rupture.** `CONTEXT_CORE_EXPANDED.md` marks that November as the corpus's `[HINGE]`: the drive home from getting his learner's permit, his father disclosing he was entering rehab at 6 a.m., his mother concurrently sleeping with their cocaine dealer, the divorce following. **Three months separate that hinge from the exit from school.** The wiki has never had the second event to put beside the first.
3. **He was still a year ahead when he left.** The cohort he was born into would have been juniors in February 2006. Leaving early from a position a year early means he finished high school at the age his own age-cohort finished eleventh grade — the offset held all the way to the end, and the dropout did not cost him it.

[[wiki/people/zachariah-harshman]]'s thread, which the wiki already reads as *"a fellow burnout reminiscing about a drug-hazed senior year,"* is a contemporaneous account of the same months from someone else who was in them. The senior year it reminisces about is the one Dan attended for roughly five months.

## Penn State Fayette — four semesters nobody knew about

This is the largest single addition on the page and the least dated. **Penn State Fayette (the Eberly Campus, in Lemont Furnace, roughly seven miles from [[wiki/places/337-saratoga-drive]]) appears nowhere in this wiki before today** — not in the timeline, not in the overview, not in the context core. Four semesters is two full academic years.

The window is tightly constrained even without a date. Keystone completion falls in 2006; the Full Sail departure is documented to September 2008. Four semesters is exactly what fits between them — Fall 2006, Spring 2007, Fall 2007, Spring 2008 — and that arithmetic is the only reason to prefer those particular terms. It is inference and is labelled as such below.

If it holds, it collides with a page. [[wiki/timeline/periods/dark-era-2007-2008]] currently reads as two undifferentiated years of opiate onset — Roxicet, then oxycodone, five months from first use to addiction, a November 2007 Las Vegas trip where dependence became undeniable, a winter 2008 heroin refusal, a documented eating disorder — with no institution, no schedule and no obligation anywhere in it. **The correction is not that the Dark Era was less dark. It is that it was concurrent with enrollment**, which makes it a period of functioning addiction rather than of collapse, and that is a materially different fact about how this person degrades. The Dark Era page carries the contradiction; the resolution needs a transcript or a date and has neither yet.

## What the sequence says as a sequence

Read end to end, the education record is **four institutions in five years after a fifth was abandoned**, and every one of them was completed:

- Laurel Highlands, twelfth grade — exited February 2006
- Keystone Online Charter — completed 2006
- Penn State Fayette — four semesters, completed or at least attended in full
- Full Sail — A.S. Recording Arts, graduated top 5% of class

Two readings compete and the corpus does not settle between them. The first is churn: a person who cannot stay anywhere, whose institutional attachments last two to four semesters each, and whose one long enrollment was the one he had to move a thousand miles for. The second is that the Full Sail outcome — top 5%, a Pro Tools HD 7 certification in February 2010 — is the only one of the four where the subject was something he actually wanted, and the pattern is not an inability to persist but an inability to persist at something uninteresting. [[wiki/mind/profile/big-five-psychometrics]]'s Intellect 95 against Conscientiousness-adjacent lows makes the second reading the more predictive one, but this page will not choose: both are consistent with the table, and the thing that would separate them is a Penn State Fayette transcript showing what he enrolled in and what happened to it.

## Falsifiers

- **A Penn State Fayette record dated outside 2006–2008** kills the derived window and, with it, the Dark Era contradiction above. It does not touch the four-semesters fact itself.
- **A Keystone completion date in 2007 rather than 2006** would mean the Fayette semesters do not fit in the gap, and something in the sequence is either out of order or overlapping.
- **Any record showing a normal-age cohort placement** — a fifth-grade class photo dated 1999–2000 rather than 1998–99, say — would break the year-early claim and, with it, this page's reading of [[wiki/timeline/events/bald-eagle-cummings]].
- **A GED rather than a diploma** would make the Keystone row wrong. The operator's account says "finishes on Keystone Online Charter school," which is a diploma-granting cyber charter, not an equivalency test; nothing else in the corpus mentions either.

## Gaps

- **Penn State Fayette is entirely undated.** Four semesters, no start, no end, no major, no completion status, no record of whether credits transferred to Full Sail. The 2006–2008 window on this page is derived from the two surrounding anchors and is the single most load-bearing inference here.
- **St. John's Byzantine is unverified and its exact name is unconfirmed.** The operator wrote "st.johns byzantine". A Byzantine Catholic parish school in the Uniontown area is plausible and geographically ordinary; nothing in `raw/` corroborates it, and no page has ever mentioned it.
- **Why he was started a year early is unknown.** Testing, a parental decision, a birthday-cutoff quirk, an older sibling's schedule — the record says only that it happened.
- **The February 2006 exit has no first-person account.** The date comes from "around president's day 2006," which is a memory of a holiday, not of a date. The Twitter archive does not reach back to 2006; the Facebook account was registered in January 2007. **The nearest contemporaneous witness is [[wiki/people/zachariah-harshman]]'s thread**, and nobody has read it for this.
- **Nothing records what he did between February 2006 and the Fayette enrollment**, if the derived window is right — six months at minimum, inside the run-up to the documented 2007 opiate onset.
- **Hatfield Elementary is named here for the first time**, and Leith-Hatfield is already the corpus's `[DOC]` location string for the family. Whether the school is the reason that place-name attaches to him has never been asked.
